Ultimate Screen Recording 0.2.0 (prototype, Apple Silicon, macOS 14+)

1. Drag "Ultimate Screen Recording" onto Applications.
2. This build is not notarized. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again.
   On macOS 15 or later: try to open it, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".
3. Grant Screen Recording, Microphone and (optionally) Camera when asked. After enabling Screen Recording, quit and reopen the app.

Free. No account. Your recordings stay on your Mac.
