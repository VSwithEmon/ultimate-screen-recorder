# Built-in music license

The eight background-music loops shipped with Ultimate Screen Recording (`apps/macos-native/Resources/Music`) are original
compositions generated by [`tools/generate_music.py`](tools/generate_music.py). Every sound is synthesised from scratch in that
script (oscillators, envelopes, noise and a synthetic reverb). **No samples, recordings or third-party material are used.**

They are dedicated to the public domain under **[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)**.
You can use, modify and redistribute them in any project, including commercial videos, without attribution or payment.
